Cluster Profile Summary
=======================
Source: KMeans clustering on customer data, visualized via heatmap (z-scores, raw means annotated).
Number of clusters: 4 | Total customers: 7,032

---

CLUSTER 0 — Loyal Premium Customers
Size: 1,758 customers
Churn rate: ~10%

Key characteristics:
Demographics:
- Low share of seniors (~16%) — younger to middle-aged customer group
- High share with partner (~73%) and relatively high share with dependents (~39%)
- Balanced gender split (51% female / 49% male)

Services & contracts:
- Longest tenure of all clusters (~59 months)
- Highest total charges (~5,410) and high monthly charges (~91)
- Fiber optic (~58%) and DSL (~42%) — none without internet
- Almost all have phone service (~95%), of which 70% have multiple lines
- All add-on services strongly activated: online security (~62%),
  backup (~71%), device protection (~74%), tech support (~67%),
  streaming TV (~76%) and movies (~77%)
- High share of two-year contracts (~53%), low share of month-to-month (~8%)

Billing & payment:
- Paperless billing ~66%
- Payment evenly split between bank transfer (~36%) and credit card (~36%);
  low share of electronic check (~20%)

Interpretation:
Established, high-investment customers with strong lock-in through long contracts and
many services. High partner share and low senior share suggest an economically active
life stage with a stable household. Despite high charges, churn is low — they are satisfied
and have a lot to lose by switching. Priority: retain with proactive support
and loyalty offers.

CLUSTER 1 — Low-Cost Customers Without Internet
Size: 1,521 customers
Churn rate: ~7%

Key characteristics:
Demographics:
- Lowest share of seniors of all clusters (~3%) — markedly younger profile
- Medium share with partner (~48%) and relatively high share with dependents (~42%)
- Balanced gender split (49% female / 51% male)

Services & contracts:
- Medium tenure (~31 months)
- Very low monthly charges (~21) and total charges (~667)
- 100% without internet — phone-only subscriptions
- All have phone service but low share of multiple lines (~22%)
- No add-on services whatsoever (streaming, security, etc. = 0%)
- Mixed contracts: two-year (~42%), month-to-month (~34%), one-year (~24%)

Billing & payment:
- Lowest paperless billing of all clusters (~29%) — prefer paper billing
- High share of mailed check (~48%); low share of electronic check (~8%)

Interpretation:
The simplest possible service — phone only, no digital add-ons. The low
senior share and high dependent share may indicate families with limited
need for broadband (e.g. children covered by mobile plans). Low churn despite
minimal engagement suggests they are satisfied. Mailed check and low paperless
billing point to customers who prefer traditional channels. Cautious upselling
of internet is possible, but avoid disrupting a stable customer group.

CLUSTER 2 — High-Risk Customers (Critical Risk Group)
Size: 2,078 customers
Churn rate: ~55%

Key characteristics:
Demographics:
- Highest share of seniors of all clusters (~30%) — clear demographic risk
- Lowest share with partner (~38%) and lowest share with dependents (~15%)
- Balanced gender split (49% female / 51% male)

Services & contracts:
- Short tenure (~20 months)
- High monthly charges (~86) but low total charges (~1,779) relative to the rate
- Almost exclusively fiber optic (99.9%), none without internet
- All have phone service (100%); 55% have multiple lines
- Low share of security services: online security (~14%), tech support (~14%)
- Moderate streaming (~45%) and backup/device protection (~30%)
- Overwhelming share of month-to-month contracts (~97%)

Billing & payment:
- Highest paperless billing (~79%)
- Dominant electronic check payers (~64%) — the strongest churn indicator
- Low share of automatic payments (bank transfer ~14%, credit card ~12%)

Interpretation:
The clearest churn signal in the dataset. Fiber optic customers without contract
lock-in who pay a lot but receive little added value (no security services, no support).
High senior share combined with single-person households (low partner and dependent share)
may indicate price-sensitive customers who actively compare alternatives. Electronic check
is a known churn indicator — manual payment increases cost awareness
every month. Highest priority for retention efforts: offer contract conversion
and bundling of add-on services.

CLUSTER 3 — DSL Customers on Monthly Contracts
Size: 1,675 customers
Churn rate: ~26%

Key characteristics:
Demographics:
- Low share of seniors (~11%)
- Lowest share with partner (~36%) and medium share with dependents (~27%)
- Balanced gender split (49% female / 51% male)

Services & contracts:
- Short tenure (~21 months)
- Medium-high monthly charges (~51) and low total charges (~1,095)
- All have DSL (100%), no fiber and no "no internet"
- Lowest share of phone service of all clusters (~65%); low share of multiple lines (~15%)
- Moderate add-on services: online security (~38%), tech support (~35%),
  backup (~33%), device protection (~29%), streaming (~26–27%)
- High share of month-to-month contracts (~72%), little two-year (~7%)

Billing & payment:
- Medium paperless billing (~55%)
- Mixed payment: electronic check (~34%) and mailed check (~31%)
- Low share of automatic payments (bank transfer ~17%, credit card ~18%)

Interpretation:
A middle category with a noticeably elevated churn risk. DSL customers with short tenures
and monthly contracts — either newly acquired customers evaluating the service or
dissatisfied customers who haven't upgraded to fiber. The lowest phone share suggests
some customers may have chosen broadband only, without voice. Mixed payment profile with
relatively high electronic check and mailed check points to manual payers with
potentially high price awareness. Retention strategy: encourage longer contracts
and more add-on services to increase stickiness.

---

OVERALL CONCLUSIONS
=======================

Churn ranking (highest to lowest):
  Cluster 2 (~55%) > Cluster 3 (~26%) > Cluster 0 (~10%) > Cluster 1 (~7%)

Strongest churn drivers identified:
  1. Fiber optic + month-to-month contract (Cluster 2)
  2. Short tenure (Clusters 2 and 3)
  3. Electronic check as payment method (Clusters 2 and 3)
  4. Absence of add-on services (Cluster 2)
  5. High senior share + low partner/dependent share (Cluster 2) — single-person households

Demographic observations:
  - Gender has no distinguishing effect — evenly distributed across all clusters
  - Seniors are heavily overrepresented in high-risk Cluster 2 (~30% vs ~3–16% in others)
  - Partner and dependents co-vary with lower churn (Clusters 0 and 1)

Recommended actions:
  - Cluster 2: Urgent retention focus — offer contract conversion and bundling;
               special attention to the senior segment
  - Cluster 3: Upgrade incentives from monthly contracts; offer DSL→fiber campaigns
  - Cluster 0: Reward loyalty, prevent passive attrition
  - Cluster 1: Cautious upselling of internet services through traditional channels
